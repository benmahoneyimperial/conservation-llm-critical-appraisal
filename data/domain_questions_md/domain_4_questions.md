Domain 4 - Performance Bias Checklist (Structured for LLM Use)

Question ID: 4.1
Category: Conditional
When to answer: If experimental treatments are applied in the study; otherwise select Not applicable
Question: Were any persons who applied or received treatments (intervention, exposure, alternative intervention, alternative exposure, or control) aware of the tested hypothesis or comparison being made?
Notes/Examples: Awareness of intervention/exposure/alternative/control treatment and outcome of interest might influence treatment procedure.
Allowed answers: Yes | Seemingly yes | Seemingly no | No | Unclear (Yes) | Not applicable

Question ID: 4.2
Category: Conditional
When to answer: If experimental treatments are applied in the study; otherwise select Not applicable
Question: Were there alterations of intervention/exposure or control treatments of interest that might affect intervention effectiveness or exposure impact?
Notes/Examples: Alterations may include deviated initiation, implementation, and/or discontinuation. E.g., select Yes or Seemingly yes if intervention/exposure start time deviates from a specified time window.
Allowed answers: Yes | Seemingly yes | Seemingly no | No | Unclear (Yes) | Not applicable

Question ID: 4.3
Category: Conditional
When to answer: If 4.2 is Yes, Seemingly yes, or Unclear; otherwise select Not applicable
Question: Were these deviated treatments unbalanced between intervention/exposure groups (for two interventions/exposures), or inaccurately taken into account (for intervention/exposure vs control), such that they might have influenced the estimated impact or effectiveness?
Notes/Examples: E.g., select Yes or Seemingly yes when nitrogen fertilizer was mistakenly applied more than initially planned for one group, but this deviation was not reflected in the data collection sheet (i.e., not occurred as recorded).
Allowed answers: Yes | Seemingly yes | Seemingly no | No | Unclear (Yes) | Not applicable

Question ID: 4.4
Category: Conditional
When to answer: If experimental treatments are applied in the study; otherwise select Not applicable
Question: Were both exposure/intervention and comparator treatments initiated and implemented as intended (or occurred as recorded) for all, or nearly all, subjects or areas?
Notes/Examples: If intervention, exposure, alternative intervention, alternative exposure, or control treatment is not successful, it might be a source of performance bias.
Allowed answers: Yes | Seemingly yes | Seemingly no | No | Unclear (No) | Not applicable

Question ID: 4.5
Category: Conditional
When to answer: If 4.2 is Yes, Seemingly yes, or Unclear, or if 4.4 is No, Seemingly no, or Unclear; otherwise select Not applicable
Question: Are analysis methods used for exposure impact or intervention effectiveness appropriate in relation to bias due to altered treatment procedures of interest?
Notes/Examples: E.g., select No or Seemingly no if altered treatment procedure is not taken into account in analysis.
Allowed answers: Yes | Seemingly yes | Seemingly no | No | Unclear (No) | Not applicable

Question ID: 4.6
Category: Optional
When to answer: Optional; assessors may skip if unfeasible. Select Not applicable if no experimental treatments are applied.
Question: What are the predicted magnitude and direction of biases due to altered treatment procedures of interest?
Notes/Examples: Quantitative assessment (e.g., simulation) may be conducted by risk-of-bias assessor(s) to predict magnitude and direction of bias for this study result.
Allowed answers: Intervention or exposure intolerably favored (*) | Intervention or exposure tolerably favored (**) | Comparator intolerably favored (*) | Comparator tolerably favored (**) | Intolerably towards no effect (*) | Tolerably towards no effect (**) | Intolerably away from no effect (*) | Tolerably away from no effect (**) | Unpredictable | Not applicable | Skip

Source note: Table B4 and (c) 2021 the authors.
Bias magnitude definitions: * Intolerable means the study result should not be considered valid enough in relation to predicted bias magnitude. ** Tolerable means the study result could be considered valid enough in relation to predicted bias magnitude.
